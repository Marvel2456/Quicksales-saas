# Quicksales User Guide

This guide helps new users access Quicksales and complete everyday tasks.

## 1) Access and Login

1. Open the application URL provided by your administrator.
2. Sign in with your email and password.
3. If prompted to change your password, complete the update and continue.

## 2) Roles and What You Can Do

- Owner: Full access to organization setup, branches, users, products, inventory, sales, and reports.
- Manager: Day-to-day operations in assigned branches, products, inventory, and sales.
- Sales: Store, cart, checkout, and sales workflows in assigned branches.

## 3) Navigation Basics

- Use the left sidebar to move between Dashboard, Category, Products, Inventory, Store, and Sales.
- The top bar shows notifications and your user menu (account and logout).

## 4) Organization and Branches

Owners can create and manage branches:

1. Go to Branch.
2. Click Add Branch.
3. Enter details and save.

## 5) Categories

Categories are scoped by branch and organization.

1. Go to Category.
2. Select a branch.
3. Click Add Category, enter a name, and save.

## 6) Products

Products are created per branch and organization.

1. Go to Products and select a branch.
2. Click Add Product.
3. Enter product details and choose a category.
4. Save the product.

Bulk upload (Owner only):

1. Go to Products.
2. Click Upload Product.
3. Upload a CSV or Excel file with product details.

## 7) Inventory

Inventory is tied to a branch.

1. Go to Inventory and select a branch.
2. Click Add Inventory.
3. Choose a product, set cost and sale price, and save.

Restocking:

1. Open a product in Inventory.
2. Click Restock and enter quantity and prices.

## 8) Store and Sales

The Store view shows available stock for a branch.

1. Go to Store and select a branch.
2. Click the cart icon on a product to add it to the sale.
3. Open Cart to adjust quantities.
4. Continue to Checkout and complete the payment.

Multiple sales sessions:

- If enabled, you can create or switch between open sales in Cart.

## 9) Sales Reports

Owners can view sales history and export reports:

1. Go to Sales and select a branch.
2. Filter by date or sales rep.
3. Export CSV or PDF when needed.

## 10) Notifications

- Notification bell shows important events.
- Open the dropdown to view recent alerts or mark them read.

## 11) Account and Profile

1. Open Account from the user menu.
2. Update profile, change password, or update organization branding.

## 12) Session Timeout

- Your session expires after 10 minutes of inactivity.
- Stay active to keep your session alive. When it expires, a prompt will appear and redirect you to login.

## 13) Common Issues and Fixes

- Missing categories: Verify you selected the correct branch.
- Cannot see products: Confirm your role and branch assignment.
- Inventory not updating: Refresh the page and confirm quantities.
- Login issues: Reset password or contact your administrator.

## 14) Best Practices

- Keep product names consistent.
- Use clear category names.
- Review inventory levels daily.
- Close completed sales to keep reports accurate.

If you need help, contact your organization owner or support team.
